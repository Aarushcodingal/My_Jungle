About the Jungle

Jungles are thick, tangled forests teeming with life!

They cover about 6% of Earth's land surface but host more than half of the world's plant and animal species. Jungles play a crucial role in regulating Earth's oxygen levels and global climate systems.

Jungle Animals

In the jungle, you can find a huge variety of unique creatures:

  • Jaguars & Tigers: Top predators that rely on camouflage to hunt.
  • Toucans & Macaws: Colorful birds adapted for cracking hard nuts and seed shells.
  • Monkeys & Sloths: Tree-dwelling animals built for climbing and foraging.

Fun Facts

  1. Rainforest trees produce so much moisture that they create their own rainfall clouds!
  2. The Amazon is the largest tropical rainforest in the world.
  3. Over 80% of the food we eat originally came from jungle environments (like chocolate, bananas, and coffee!).
